Privacy Policy

EslabQol: Inglizcha so‘zlar — a free, offline English vocabulary learning app for Uzbek speakers.

Effective date: 20 November 2026 App version: 1.0.0 Android package: uz.sozatlas.app

The short version: EslabQol does not ask you to create an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not collect or send your personal information anywhere. Everything you do in the app is saved only on your own device. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no cloud services.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the mobile application EslabQol: Inglizcha so‘zlar (package name uz.sozatlas.app), published on Google Play. It explains what the app does with information on your device. It does not cover other apps or websites that you may open from the Android share sheet or your browser; those have their own privacy policies.

2. Information the app stores on your device

The app keeps all of its data in a private area of your device that Android reserves for this app. No other app can normally read it. The data falls into these groups:

  • Vocabulary and collections: the words included with the app, plus any words, word lists, and collections you add yourself.
  • Your learning progress: which words you have studied, how many answers you got right or wrong, review dates and spaced-repetition state, daily exercise counts, your streak, favorite words, and goal progress.
  • Your own notes: if you choose to write personal notes on a word, those notes are saved with that word on your device.
  • Your optional display name: if you choose to enter a name in the Profile screen, it is saved locally. It is only shown back to you in the app; it is not required and is never sent anywhere.
  • Settings and preferences: theme (light, dark, or system), daily goal, learning language preferences, automatic pronunciation and haptic-feedback switches, whether onboarding was completed, and your daily-reminder setting (on/off and chosen time).

Learning data and vocabulary are stored in a local SQLite database; settings and preferences are stored with Android's SharedPreferences. None of this information leaves your device.

3. Information the app does not collect

To be clear, EslabQol does not collect or transmit:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details (other than the optional name you type into your own device);
  • account or login information — there are no accounts and no sign-in;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • photos, videos, audio recordings, or microphone input;
  • contacts, call logs, or messages;
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs, or browsing history;
  • payment, purchase, or subscription information — the app is free and has no in-app purchases or ads;
  • analytics, crash reports, usage statistics, or behavioral profiles.

4. Internet access and third-party services

The release version of EslabQol is fully offline. It does not request the Android INTERNET permission, does not open network connections, and does not send data to any server. (Debug and profile builds used during development contain Flutter's standard INTERNET permission for tooling such as hot reload; those builds are not the versions distributed on Google Play.)

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no tracking tools, no artificial-intelligence APIs, and no cloud storage or sync. It does not sell, share, or disclose your information to third parties, because it never receives information that could be sent.

The libraries the app uses are local utilities only: state management, local database and preferences storage, on-device text-to-speech, the Android system share sheet, local notification scheduling, timezone handling, and opening this policy page in your browser. None of them receives your learning data from EslabQol.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13+): used only for the optional daily study reminder described in section 6. The app asks for this permission only if you turn the reminder on. If you say no, the rest of the app keeps working normally.
  • No other sensitive permissions: the app does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage/file access, phone, or device-identity permissions.
  • The app also declares "queries" so Android can find the device's text-to-speech service and other apps that handle plain text. These are not permissions and do not give the app access to your data.

6. Notifications

The daily reminder is optional and switched off by default. If you enable it in Settings or in the Profile screen, the app schedules one reminder per day at the time you choose, using your device's local time zone. The notification is created entirely on your device — it is not a push notification and no message or data is sent to or received from a server. You can change the time, turn the reminder off at any time in the app, or disable notifications for the app in Android Settings.

7. Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

The pronounce button uses your device's built-in, offline text-to-speech engine to speak English words aloud. The word text is processed on your device; no audio or text is uploaded. If an English voice is not installed on your device, pronunciation may be unavailable, but the rest of the app continues to work.

8. Clipboard, import, export, and sharing

  • Export: when you choose to export your vocabulary as JSON or CSV, the app builds the file contents locally, copies them to your device's clipboard, and opens the Android share sheet so you can decide what to do with them. An export happens only when you tap the export button.
  • Clipboard: because exported data is copied to the system clipboard, other apps or services that you allow to read the clipboard may be able to see it. You can clear your clipboard at any time. The app does not read your clipboard for any other purpose.
  • Import: to import words, you paste JSON or CSV text into a dialog in the app. It is validated and saved locally. Nothing is uploaded, and the app does not need a cloud account or file-download access to import. Duplicate words and collections are skipped or merged rather than duplicated.
  • Sharing: after a study session, or when exporting, the app can open the standard Android share sheet with a short text (for example, your session result or exported vocabulary). The app does not choose where the text goes, does not send it itself, and does not record which app you pick. Sharing is always your choice and is never automatic. Once text leaves EslabQol through the share sheet, it is handled by the app you choose and by that app's privacy practices.

9. How to delete your data

  • Clear learning history: in the app, open Settings and choose "O‘rganish tarixini tozalash" ("Clear learning history"). This deletes your results, streak, and review schedule. It does not delete your vocabulary, your personal notes, your optional display name, or your settings.
  • Remove individual content: you can edit or clear a word's personal notes in the word details screen, and you can clear or change your display name in the Profile screen.
  • Remove everything: uninstalling EslabQol from your device deletes all app data stored on that device, including vocabulary, notes, name, settings, and the scheduled reminder.

EslabQol disables Android's automatic backup (allowBackup="false"), so your learning data is not copied to Google Drive or another cloud backup by the app. There is no online account to delete and no server-side copy of your data. If you want a personal copy before uninstalling, use the export feature in section 8.

10. Data security

Your data stays inside Android's app-specific sandbox for EslabQol and is not transmitted, so there is no network transfer to encrypt or intercept. We do not store passwords or payment details. As with any app, keeping your device locked and up to date helps protect data stored on it. Be aware that exported text you copy to the clipboard or send through another app is outside EslabQol's control.

11. Children's privacy

EslabQol is a general-audience educational app. It does not collect any personal information, does not show ads, does not build profiles, and contains no external links except this policy page when you open it. A child using the app only creates local learning data on the device, which the device owner can remove by uninstalling the app. The app does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

12. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be changed. The current version performs no network communication. The app includes a link to this page so you can always read the latest version.
